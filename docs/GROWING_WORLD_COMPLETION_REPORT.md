inverse sharing, unit-square area and principal square sides. Pictures expose the
relationships before formal symbols. The original eight families remain reachable
by explicit choices; Shapes and measurement are independent paths. There is no
global level, age ceiling, access lock or transferred mastery.

The child-visible discovery board offers a suggested next opportunity and a
reason, alongside every chapter and familiar activity. Suggestions come from
actual synthetic evidence, independently of Star/Triangle identity. The separate
experimental chapter policy requires four distinct independent cases, two
representations and two explicit sessions for a connected invitation. Legacy
addition/length can bridge after three distinct independent cases without a
retention claim. Requested support, wrong attempts and worked explanations remain
distinct from independent evidence. Repeated translated or transformed cases do
not add distinct independent cases. A bounded, fresh, correct, unassisted later
session can confirm missing representation/session coverage without creating a
new case. Four initial successes in one sitting cannot permanently trap growth.
Later-session recovery remains possible. Accomplishments
are preserved; no time/speed/reward mechanism pressures return.

Explore has two reversible workshops. Move tiles between parts while preserving
the whole, change the whole, add/remove rows and columns, turn arrays, make equal
sides, reveal symbols, undo and reset. Equal groups connect to area and square
side relationships. Explore updates local workspace memory only; it submits no
answers, writes no observations and changes no readiness. A separate practice
button generates a fresh assessment opportunity. Workspace state is isolated per
badge within the session and language changes preserve the experiment.

session upgrades to
`growing-world-synthetic-loop-v2` without losing prior events, derived state,

The repository envelope namespace stays stable; the new reader descriptor

product build.

Mathematical help is saved before it is revealed in assessed activities. A

## Playable surface and walkthrough

Use the pinned process-local Corepack settings in [development](DEVELOPMENT.md),
then run `corepack pnpm exec vite build --config vite.slice.config.ts`, followed by
`corepack pnpm exec vite preview --config vite.slice.config.ts --host 127.0.0.1 --port 4178 --strictPort`.
Open [the synthetic experience](http://127.0.0.1:4178/). This is an explicit local
synthetic capability, not a public deployment or ordinary-product persistence.

1. Choose Ελληνικά, English or Deutsch and choose a badge.
2. Choose whether practice should guide invitations with the visible button, then
   choose a discovery such as Make a ten or Build equal groups. Just play remains
   available, and choosing an activity preserves that explicit mode.
3. Ask Show me repeatedly to organise the relationship and reveal an explicitly
   worked explanation. Continue or follow a connected discovery separately.
4. Use Stop and return in a new session. Distinct independent experiences across
   representations/sessions can invite a related idea; help instead invites
   support. Grown-ups shows precise experimental evidence and its limits.
5. Choose Explore, move a tile, turn an array, undo, and reveal a square's side.
   Return to Play or start separate practice; exploration earns no assessed claim.
6. Switch language/badge, reopen, and use the verified cached local surface.
