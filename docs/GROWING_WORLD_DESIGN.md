## Connected chapter

Six separately identified bounded families connect making ten and missing parts
to equal groups and their inverse, rectangular unit-square area, and square side
length/principal square roots. The original eight families and immutable replay
meanings remain. The new content reaches totals beyond ten but the substantive
growth is decomposition, inverse and equal-unit relationships. Geometry, unit
length, area and square-side opportunities remain independently selectable;
there is no arithmetic access gate or global level.

| Family                   | Bounded mathematical meaning                                                                            |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `number.make-ten`        | First part 6–9 and a second part crossing ten; choose the amount that fills ten, then see the remainder |
| `number.part-whole`      | Missing part in totals 11–20, with concrete and relational representations                              |
| `groups.equal`           | 2–5 equal groups of 2–5; repeated addition and multiplication                                           |
| `groups.missing`         | Exact inverse: total and number of groups determine group size, no remainder                            |
| `measurement.array-area` | 2–5 rows/columns of equal unit squares; area has square-unit dimension                                  |
| `powers.square-side`     | Principal nonnegative side of perfect squares 1–36, distinct from solving a squared equation            |

New generator/content/semantic IDs and separate bounded exact DTOs identify the
chapter. Truth and transitions belong to pure domain modules. UI diagrams,
translations and speech cannot decide correctness. Independent finite-domain
enumeration and meaningful restored mutations will review those contracts.

## Experimental invitations and saved compatibility

The historical three-scope Phase 3B catalogue and observation replay remain
frozen. A separately named experimental invitation policy derives new chapter
opportunities. Four distinct independent semantic cases, two representations
and two explicit sessions are a conservative engineering hypothesis for a
chapter invitation, not validated mastery or delayed retention. Familiar
addition can invite making ten after several distinct independent cases.
Assisted/worked answers, retries, translated/representation repetitions and one
lucky answer cannot create distinct independent mathematical cases. A fresh,
correct, unassisted first attempt in a later explicit session may confirm missing
representation/session coverage at most once per case/representation. It creates
no new case, so exhausting a finite scope in one sitting cannot freeze future
invitations. Same-session repetitions and helped confirmations cannot supply
that coverage. Accessibility alternatives
are not mathematical hints. Support signals invite another representation,
familiar practice, Explore or stopping, without removing accomplishments.

New chapter evidence keeps `limitedEvidence` and explicit uncertainty. The
child-facing journey exposes its invitation and reason, all opportunities remain
selectable, and the finite chapter edge is visible. Badge identity never controls
mathematical readiness. The policy is synthetic and proposed; no educational
effectiveness or real-use policy approval follows.

The existing repository envelope remains a stable namespace. A new discriminated
payload schema holds the bounded chapter journal while the new codec preserves
exact legacy v1 bytes. Entering the new chapter or explicitly requesting help
in an assessed bridge family upgrades without
discarding preferences, old events/derived state, progress or integrity metadata.
Legacy pending commands settle under their original semantics. The learner-reader
compatibility descriptor changes so an old shell cannot claim to read new payloads.
Epoch/revision validation, atomic receipts, failed-save recovery and profile
isolation remain mandatory.

Requested mathematical help has a separate bounded semantic support context,
saved before the help is revealed. It survives badge switches, activity changes
and reopening in the same synthetic session. Answer preparation and pending
decoding apply the maximum saved tier even if a caller underreports assistance;
the chapter's worked tier forces solution exposure. This metadata is not an
assessment event or mastery evidence. An explicit new v2 session clears the
context and advances to a fresh replay; it claims no time-based retention.
The shared synthetic receipt limit is 4,096 to cover the bounded combined
histories and support writes, with no receipt pruning or real-use retention rule.

## Explore

A number workshop changes totals 0–20 and moves units between two parts. An array
workshop changes rows and columns 1–5, turns the arrangement, compares equal
totals and constructs squares. Both use discrete accessible buttons, undo and
reset. Concrete diagrams precede symbols. The square root view retains a single
top-side bracket and highlights only that side. Actions are session-only and
emit no assessment events, readiness changes or negative evidence. A deliberate
separate practice action generates a fresh task; discoveries are never converted
retroactively into assessment.
