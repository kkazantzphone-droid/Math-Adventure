# Project vision

Status: Phase 0 specification, 2026-10-04. No feature is implemented.

Math Adventure began as a personal mathematics game for two six-year-old children, one especially interested in numbers. Its durable purpose is to invite mathematical curiosity through puzzles, representations, spoken help and independent learner pathways. The working title remains provisional.

The initial experience should support early-primary learners without demanding fluent reading. Plan first-class pathways for number sense, arithmetic, patterns/sequences, multiplication/division, geometry/spatial reasoning, measurement, exponentiation/roots, fractions, decimals/percentages, algebra, logic and probability/combinatorics. Geometry begins with early shape/spatial ideas; measurement and powers/roots have conceptual progressions, not a miscellaneous-extension bucket. A learner can be advanced in arithmetic and developing in spatial reasoning. Shared concepts connect domains without a global math level or artificial silos. Demonstrated readiness, prerequisites and voluntary exploration matter more than age labels.

Number Lab should allow safe bounded exploration of large numbers, multiplication, squares, powers, roots and patterns beyond recommendations. Exploratory exposure never implies demonstrated mastery. Domain truth remains exact or explicitly approximate; language and rendering cannot redefine it.

Success means a child can understand an invitation, try ideas, receive useful help, stop comfortably and return to their own progress. A parent can explain a recommendation and control local data. Contributors can prove correctness and review language/accessibility without understanding an elaborate platform. Enjoyment and usefulness must be evaluated with consented, proportionate human observation; they are goals, not research outcomes already established.

The application is a learning/exploration tool, not a diagnostic, intelligence, grading or surveillance system. It cannot infer developmental, neurological or medical conditions. No advertising, sibling comparison or pressure to maintain engagement is part of the vision.
