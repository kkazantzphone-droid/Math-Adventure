# Owner decisions before real household saved/adaptive use

Prepared 2026-10-18 (Europe/Athens) for Clear answers & coherent discovery.
This summarizes existing decisions; it accepts no proposal and authorizes no
real-learner use. The current saved experience uses fixed synthetic profiles in
an explicit local review capability. Ordinary-product learner persistence remains
disabled. Engineering delivery and protected merge do not supply owner UX
acceptance, educational validation or permission to start a household trial.

## Already decided

- **Local, minimal, separate profiles:** V1 has no learner accounts, backend,
  cloud learner store or remote telemetry. Profiles have independent evidence;
  there is no sibling ranking or global math level. Local storage is neither
  authentication nor an automatic encryption/backup promise
  ([ADR-0003](adr/ADR-0003.md), [privacy policy](CHILD_SAFETY_AND_PRIVACY.md)).
- **Honest evidence:** mathematical correctness belongs to the domain. Supported
  and worked answers cannot become independent evidence through navigation or
  reopening. Explore stays unassessed. Current chapter invitations are separately
  versioned experiments, and new families retain limited-evidence qualifications
  ([Growing World design](GROWING_WORLD_DESIGN.md)).
- **Language and speech boundaries:** UI, instructions and number speech have
  independent language roles. Greek, British English and German remain drafts.
  Speech is optional, explicitly played and exact-region/local-only; a provider's
  local flag does not prove offline playback ([ADR-0012](adr/ADR-0012.md)).
- **Private real data stays outside public evidence:** repository fixtures,
  reports, screenshots and PR attachments must be synthetic. A later private
  evaluation needs its own authorized consent/privacy workflow
  ([ADR-0008](adr/ADR-0008.md)).

## Decisions and evidence still needed

| Existing question | What needs to be settled before the relevant real-use claim/action |
| --- | --- |
| **Retention and deletion — Q03, D19–D20** | Owner plus privacy review must decide observation limits, profile/summary inactivity period, renewal, grace, final deletion and clock-uncertainty notices. The proposed 10 observations/concept, 500/profile, 60 days and 12-month inactivity limit are not accepted defaults. Review checkpoint lifetime and a receipt-retirement design that preserves exact retries; synthetic journal/receipt capacities are not retention policy. |
| **Private household scope and controls — Q04–Q05, D21** | Owner plus qualified privacy/legal advice must establish the intended operator/household role, territories, purposes/basis, notices, rights, applicable storage-access requirements and assessment needs. Decide adaptation defaults and meaningful adult/manual controls; local deterministic adaptation does not itself settle profiling or storage-access questions. Authorize any real-data implementation/trial explicitly after that review. |
| **Educational interpretation — Q08, D18 / proposed ADR-0006** | Independent educators/math/accessibility reviewers must evaluate concept/representation scope, finite-case diversity, invitation thresholds, support/revisit rules and fair opportunities. Agree any consented evaluation plan. Synthetic passing scenarios do not validate mastery, transfer, retention or learning effectiveness. |
| **Accessible use on intended devices — Q10/Q13/Q16, D23/D25/D29/D30** | Choose supported browser/OS/device surfaces and obtain scoped accessible-geometry, notation, input, storage and optional-voice evidence. Historical D53 owner observations establish only their bound environment; changed screens need current evidence. Automated reflow and browser-offline tests do not establish actual AT delivery or physical disconnection. |
| **Wording — Q09, D22** | Obtain proportionate native-language and spoken-wording review for the intended household use. Official pack promotion additionally needs the specified reviewer arrangements; schema completeness is insufficient. |
| **Dependable saved state — Q12, D24** | Before relying on backup or migration, implement and review parent-controlled local export/import and recovery on intended surfaces, including deletion fences and failure paths. Decide the practical response to eviction, cleared storage and private exports that outlive app deletion. Existing synthetic persistence proof is bounded engineering evidence. |
