# Research evidence ledger

Access date for every source below: **2026-10-04**. Research was read-only public browsing by the principal agent and independent research reviewers. No source content was uploaded with learner data. This is a bounded review, not a systematic review or compliance certification.

**VERIFIED FACT** means the identified source was inspected and supports the limited claim. **ARCHITECTURAL INFERENCE** is this project's interpretation, not a source conclusion. **RECOMMENDATION** is a proposed choice. **UNRESOLVED QUESTION** denotes missing verification/calibration. Sources were paraphrased; vendor capability claims do not certify our devices or deployment.
