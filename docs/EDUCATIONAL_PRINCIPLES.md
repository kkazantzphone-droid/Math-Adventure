| Principle | Required design consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Ability before age | Prerequisite graph and evidence govern recommendations; no school-year gate or age ceiling. Independent related domains replace a global math level; exploration can bypass recommendation readiness. |
| Independent learners | Separate evidence and preferences. No sibling ranking, public scores or comparative mastery display. |
| Mistakes as information | Offer another representation/help; no lives, shame, red failure screen, loss of rewards or game-over penalty. |
| Reading independence | Speakable prompts with accessible visual/action equivalents; numerical readiness cannot be inferred from reading fluency. |
| Speed is not intelligence | Untimed core play. Response time is absent from V1 mastery and access decisions. Future timing needs a specific pedagogical review. |
| Explainable adaptation | Show evidence, uncertainty and reason codes, not a mysterious score or diagnosis. |
| Deterministic truth | Domain logic owns answers; language, speech, UI and AI cannot alter correctness. |
| Curiosity | Number Lab and optional harder trials; discoveries and reversible experiments are valuable without scoring. |
| Productive struggle | Child-controlled cues, representations, guided explanation and worked examples. No compulsory waiting for help. |
| Comfortable stopping | No streak anxiety, countdowns, FOMO, notifications, ads, paid randomness or infinite reward manipulation. |

Use concrete quantities, pictures and symbols as connected alternatives. Do not force a universal one-way concrete-to-abstract ladder or interpret preference for a representation as a deficit. Review object placement, countability, symbol conventions and misleading distractors.

Phase 1V's authorized founder-family findings justify a bounded concrete-first exploration presentation: sixteen unit tiles remain visible while multiplication, square power and principal-root symbols highlight the same relationship. The final root view highlights only four top tiles under one bracket labelled 4; concrete square/side meaning precedes the concluding √16 = 4. Symbols are discoveries, not prerequisites, tests or evidence of understanding. Same-shape matching and more discoverable child-controlled help address interface findings without inferring ability or changing mathematical difficulty. **FINAL PASS — OWNER CONFIRMED** records acceptance of Space, Shapes, Show me, Explore and the final root view. This bounded project design input is not representative preference research, educational-effectiveness evidence, accessibility certification or mathematical-ability evidence. No learner/evidence or exploratoryExposure event is created by this prototype.

Geometry/spatial reasoning, measurement and exponentiation/roots are first-class planned pathways alongside number sense, arithmetic, patterns/sequences, multiplication/division, fractions, decimals/percentages, algebra, logic and probability/combinatorics. Shape classification is not gated by unrelated arithmetic performance. Link arrays to multiplication, square areas to square numbers, powers to repeated multiplication, roots to inverse powers, fractions to area and measurement to arithmetic. Cross-domain prerequisites need concept-specific evidence/rationale, not a demand to finish a whole domain.

For powers/roots, connect repeated multiplication, squares/cubes and inverse relationships before treating notation as fluency. In Number Lab, seeing or hearing a power/root is exploratory exposure; require distinct interpretable independent evidence before a mastery claim. Bounded safe exploration remains available without performance pressure.

Progressive help is semantic: cue attention, expose a useful relationship, provide a visual/spoken strategy, guide a step, then demonstrate an example. A hint must not change the underlying problem silently. Worked success records supported experience, not independent mastery. A child may skip, ask for help or return to easier exploration.

Positive rewards may acknowledge trying strategies, finding relationships and personal milestones. Reward state has no authority over mathematical access or learner evidence. Avoid praise that claims fixed ability. Parent labels are Emerging, Developing and Secure, plus insufficient-evidence and revisit indicators; these describe observed task evidence only.
