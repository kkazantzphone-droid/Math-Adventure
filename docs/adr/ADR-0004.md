# ADR-0004: Deterministic truth and seeded puzzles

Status: **ACCEPTED** · Date: 2026-10-04 · Phase: 0 (specification only)

## Context

Generated puzzles, hints and validators need independently verifiable mathematical truth and replay across UI/language/platform changes, including exact future numeric domains.

## Decision

Use language-neutral semantic tasks, typed answer contracts, safe integer bounds now and exact rational/decimal types before corresponding content. Seeds, canonical specs, immutable generator/RNG/content versions and bounded generation enable replay. UI/AI/TTS/rewards cannot decide correctness. Internal family modules expose hints/accessibility/evidence scope; RNG algorithm detail remains proposed.

Include first-class semantic geometry/measurement and conceptual powers/roots: exact logical shapes/units, inclusive classification, typed expression/inverse links, perfect-root truth and bounded computation. Distinguish principal square roots from equation solution sets and exact from approximate measures. SVG+semantic HTML is the preferred proposed geometry presentation; notation options are evaluated without implementation. Related domain links do not automatically transfer mastery; Number Lab exposure remains unscored.

## Alternatives considered

Math.random/clock-derived truth cannot replay. Floating epsilon cannot validate exact fractions. LLM-generated truth is prohibited. A general CAS/dynamic plugin framework is unnecessary now.

## Consequences

Separate presentation randomness and mathematical truth. Keep versioned golden fixtures/minimal semantic diagnostics and independent oracle tests.

## Risks

Shared generator/validator bugs, unsafe intermediate arithmetic, ambiguous pattern rules and changing old generator behavior. Retired replay formats must be labelled honestly.

## Reversal conditions

New domain-specific exact/approximate types may extend contracts with proof/tests. Any loss of determinism or external truth authority requires explicit review and cannot weaken accepted principles silently.
