# ADR-0006: Explainable adaptive-learning strategy

Status: **PROPOSED** · Date: 2026-10-04 · Phase: 0 (specification only)

## Context

Readiness rather than age/speed must guide independent learners; limited evidence, help, exploration and time away must not become punitive scores.

## Decision

Recommend a deterministic versioned rule state machine with scoped Unseen/Emerging/Developing/Secure evidence, separate support/revisit/uncertainty flags, reason codes and child/manual overrides. Numeric full-window thresholds, delayed retrieval/diversity, staged revisit dates and repetition caps are unvalidated hypotheses specified in the adaptive model. No clinical claims or response-time gates.

Model independent but related pathways across number sense/arithmetic/patterns/multiplication, geometry/spatial reasoning, measurement, powers/roots, fractions/decimals, algebra, logic and probability/combinatorics, with canonical per-concept evidence and no global math level. Cross-domain relationships are not promotions. ExploratoryExposure is distinct from eligibleLearningObservation; bounded Number Lab/exploration memory never updates formal mastery by default.

## Alternatives considered

Simple thresholds alone miss representations/duplicates; weights imply arbitrary precision. Bayesian/BKT needs calibration; Elo-like ordering flattens dimensions and needs calibrated items. Opaque ML adds unjustified privacy/explanation complexity.

## Consequences

Parents can inspect why a task is offered; synthetic scenario tests are practical. Exploration/Number Lab and inaccessible tasks are neutral; support does not erase accomplishments. Finite concepts must be able to attain stated evidence criteria.

## Risks

Incorrect eligibility, chance success, unattainable diversity, unfair selection, unsupported threshold validity and profiling legal scope.

## Reversal conditions

Change rules/version after educator review, simulations and proportionate evaluation. A more complex model needs demonstrated benefit, calibration/privacy analysis and equally understandable explanations; it cannot become mathematical authority.
