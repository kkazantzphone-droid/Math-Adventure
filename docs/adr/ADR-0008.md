# ADR-0008: Public repository and synthetic data

Status: **ACCEPTED** · Date: 2026-10-04 · Phase: 0 (specification only)

## Context

The project may become public. Real learner histories, media, exports, credentials and local artifacts must never be exposed through commits or contribution channels.

## Decision

Only visibly synthetic learner/history fixtures are permitted. Use dedicated synthetic directories, defensive ignore rules, human tracked-file/asset review and future privacy/secret scans. Bug reports use standalone synthetic seed/spec reproductions. No real child screenshots/audio/exports or secrets. No publication/push/PR occurs in Phase0.

## Alternatives considered

A private folder name or .gitignore alone cannot detect all leaks. Scrubbed real exports are risky and unnecessary. Real telemetry/recordings are not acceptable test fixtures.

## Consequences

Contributor guidance, evidence provenance and artifact hygiene are quality gates. The owner selected Apache-2.0 for original code and project documentation on 2026-10-05; third-party assets retain their own terms and require separate rights review. Public publication remains subject to contribution sign-off and governance gates.

## Risks

Already tracked ignored files, screenshots/PR attachments, ambiguous fictional names, copied logs and false-positive/negative automated scans.

## Reversal conditions

Do not weaken the real-data repository prohibition. Any research collecting private data needs a separate consent/privacy workflow outside the public repository, not an exception hidden in fixtures.
